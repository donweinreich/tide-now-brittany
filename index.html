Official SHOM Predictions

Predicted High/Low Tides & Daily Chart

Live official predictions embedded directly from SHOM's free public widget (services.data.shom.fr) — includes 7 days of predicted high (PM) and low (BM) tide times with heights and coefficients, plus SHOM's own interactive tide-height chart for the day. Rendered in French by SHOM (no English version is offered by this widget). This is separate from the "Tide Now" panel above, which uses live observed data rather than predictions.

"Tide Now" source: real-time observation data from the REFMAR network (SHOM), free access, no key required — data.shom.fr. Heights are relative to zéro hydrographique (SHOM's standard chart datum, close to but not technically identical to "Mean Low Water"). SHOM's paid prediction API was not used anywhere in this page; the official predictions above are pulled from SHOM's free embeddable widget instead (maree.shom.fr), and the "next tide change" estimate in the Tide Now panel is a rough approximation from observed trend, independent of that official widget. "Tide Now" auto-refreshes every 10 minutes.
