"Tide Now" source: real-time observation data from the REFMAR network (SHOM), free access, no key required — data.shom.fr. Heights are relative to zéro hydrographique (SHOM's standard chart datum, close to but not technically identical to "Mean Low Water"). The "next tide change" estimate is a rough approximation from the observed trend, not an official SHOM prediction. Auto-refreshes every 15 minutes.
